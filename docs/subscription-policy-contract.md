# Subscription lifecycle policy and v1 contract

This contract unblocks SUB-02 through SUB-11. The defaults below are proposed implementation defaults derived from the requested lifecycle; they have not been explicitly approved as commercial terms. Keep enforcement feature-gated and make policy injectable until an operator has configured the deployment. Do not interpret this contract as authority to charge, email, migrate production data, or suspend existing organizations.

## Policy defaults

- The plan's recurring subscription fee and its included usage-credit allowance are separate amounts. The initial quote charges `terms.subscriptionPrice`; a successful paid-period activation issues exactly the snapshotted `terms.includedCredits`. An allowance such as ₹4,000 is not the fee unless the plan explicitly prices it that way.
- Use INR for the initial rollout. Carry amounts as `{ asset, units: decimal-string, scale }`; never use floating point for financial arithmetic. Snapshot plan version, all purchased terms, fee, credits, tax, and period dates into the quote/period. A later plan edit cannot alter those snapshots.
- A new unpaid organization has billing-recovery access only. It may authenticate to complete billing, but receives no operational CRM access and no subscription credit grant. Trial access must be explicitly represented as a separate approved policy.
- Send the first renewal reminder seven calendar days before the paid period's end. Renewal can be quoted at that point and remains available overdue. The proposed suspension deadline is seven calendar days after the period end, giving 14 days from the first notice. At that deadline an unpaid organization enters billing-only recovery. Existing add-on billing cycles are independent.
- Payment submission or a pending review does not count as payment and does not extend service. The default review hold is zero days; any future hold must be an explicit, bounded deployment policy, not an inference from a submitted receipt.
- A grace request does not extend access until approved. Proposed default is one request and at most seven cumulative calendar days per period, configurable by platform policy. Approval can extend access only for the billing cause. A platform operator may revoke it with a reason. Grace is not a paid period and issues no subscription credits.
- A renewal paid before expiry is scheduled contiguously at the existing period end and retains its original calendar anchor. A late approved renewal starts at payment approval time, with no retroactive access or credits for the gap; that new period becomes the next anchor. Existing `periodMath` follows this late-payment rule.
- Upgrades default to immediate effective time, with an explicit next-renewal option. Immediate charge is the positive difference between the target and current recurring fee, multiplied by the exact remaining-period milliseconds divided by total-period milliseconds, rounded half-up once to the currency's minor unit. Incremental included credits use the same fraction and rounding. Quote snapshots freeze the calculation inputs. Downgrades take effect on renewal and require the operator to resolve any seat, workspace, or feature inventory conflict before approval.
- Period dates are UTC instants and intervals are half-open. Calendar month/year arithmetic retains the original local anchor day and clamps to the last day of shorter months (Jan 31 → Feb 28/29 → Mar 31); DST boundaries use the explicit snapshotted IANA billing timezone. Default zone is UTC. Never derive a billing date from the viewer's timezone or the machine's local timezone.

## State dimensions

Period state is `awaiting_payment`, `scheduled`, `active`, `expired`, or `cancelled`. Payment review is a separate workflow using existing payment states (`submitted`, `needs_information`, `approved`, `rejected`) and fulfillment (`scheduled`, `fulfilled`). Grace state is `requested`, `approved`, `denied`, `revoked`, or `expired`. Access is computed independently as `operational`, `billing_recovery`, `billing_suspended`, `manual_suspended`, or `security_suspended`. Billing approval/grace can resolve only `billing_suspended`; it must not clear a manual or security suspension.

Access precedence: security/manual suspension wins; otherwise a current paid period, the configured seven-day post-expiry payment window, or approved, unexpired additional grace allows operational access; otherwise allow only the billing recovery surface. Pending payment review and pending grace do not change this decision. The post-expiry window is policy time, not a funded period and issues no credits.

## Commands, identity, permissions and events

The exported v1 validators are in `src/billing/contracts/subscription.js`. Every command receives trusted `operationContext` separately from its DTO. The server supplies organization identity from the authenticated membership and checks it against the requested resource. The client cannot supply actor, role, approver or organization authority. Billing administrators may create/accept organization quotes and submit payment/grace requests. Only authorized platform billing operators may approve/reject payments or approve/deny/revoke grace. Expected entity versions and operation IDs provide optimistic concurrency and idempotency.

Commands are `CreateInitialPurchaseQuote`, `CreateRenewalQuote`, `CreateUpgradeQuote`, `SubmitGraceRequest`, `DecideGraceRequest`, and `RevokeGrace`. Their validators require plan IDs and versions for initial/upgrade quotes, the target period for renewals, and reason plus expected version for grace decisions. Quote acceptance/payment proof continues through the existing payment submission contract; do not create a parallel payment ledger.

Events are versioned and tenant-scoped: `SubscriptionPurchaseQuoted.v1`, `SubscriptionPaymentSubmitted.v1`, `SubscriptionPaymentApproved.v1`, `SubscriptionPeriodScheduled.v1`, `SubscriptionPeriodActivated.v1`, `SubscriptionRenewalDue.v1`, `SubscriptionAccessRestricted.v1`, `SubscriptionAccessRestored.v1`, `SubscriptionGraceRequested.v1`, `SubscriptionGraceApproved.v1`, `SubscriptionGraceDenied.v1`, `SubscriptionGraceRevoked.v1`, `SubscriptionPlanChangeScheduled.v1`, and `SubscriptionPlanChanged.v1`. Persisted event payloads must include immutable subject IDs, effective timestamps, actor/reason for operator decisions, and relevant quote/period versions. Register consumers before publishing lifecycle events.

## Injectable runtime contract

`src/billing/modules/subscriptions/policy.js` exports `DEFAULT_SUBSCRIPTION_POLICY`, `resolveSubscriptionPolicy(overrides)`, and `createSubscriptionClock(now)`. Jobs/services must receive the resolved policy and clock; they must not read wall time directly or define independent warning, grace, or upgrade constants. The default object does not itself enable any scheduler or enforcement path.

The plan's initial purchase amount is read from the versioned plan terms' `subscriptionPrice`. Included credits are read independently from `includedCredits`. If the admin plan editor has a distinct monthly-credit field, SUB-02 must explicitly map it into versioned terms before quoting; never infer the fee from that field.
